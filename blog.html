May 14th, 2025 — by Jackson

on "Slowburn"

It's pretty exciting to have our fist song out, and with no lack of content surrounding it. We have it launched on all platforms and even have a music video. Recently we passed 2k streams on spotify, a number we weren't dreaming of hitting. So it's been pretty exciting to see it go so well.

We're hoping to have a big summer for the band full of shows and music. We even have merch on the way and it's about time. Overall the band's feeling optimistic after this release and we hope people like our next song as well.

Slowburn itself is actually one of the first songs that LSS wrote, with Grey doing the heavy lifting. It's got a super cool vibe about it just musically and something I wouldn't have thought of writing myself. The lyrics are all about letting yourself down and instead of dealing with it simply excusing your own actions only to end up doing the same thing again with the same outcome. Maybe a little depressing, but hey, we have happy songs too, just not out yet. Anyways, thanks for reading, hope you enjoy the song :) listen to it Here.

May 14th, 2025 — by Jackson

on "Web Dev"

A couple weeks ago me and the bassist, Jack, decided we needed a website. Most bands have linktrees and that does just fine, maybe even better for music discovery. But I have to say an html website is much more fun. This page is totally ours and totally editable to our liking which is cool, I think.

The thing that inspired me to make this page is the Indie Web. I don't even know that much about it because it is so new to me, but its basically this idea that instead of having these social media companies rob our attention spans while they collect all our data, lets just make simple websites and link to eachother. It's pretty freeing in my opinion.

Our page isn't perfect and probably never will be but the point is it's ours. Through time I'll add a links page and start linking to other cool websites I think people should check out.

Because this is so new to me, please email the band HERE with any cool ideas you have for the website, could be literally anything. More to come, stay tuned.

— Jackson
