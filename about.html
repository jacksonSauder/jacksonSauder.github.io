About

:: band bio ::

Luxury Sport Sedan formed in Vancouver, British Columbia is 2025. We play music we enjoy and love to hang with Vancouver's awesome music scence. We play loud grunge inspired alt rock and have a hard time picking what to say when people ask our influences.

The band formed in July 2025 though the idea was around for a lot longer. Grey and Jackson got together and wrote some material, but sat on it for a while waiting for the right guys. Jack and Ryan Pak then joined the band and we were off to the races. Eventually Ryan had to leave to focus on his UKG Dj dreams which is also dope (@socialclub_ca on instagram) and Matt joined the band. Matt is an absolute wizard in the studio and mixed and mastered slowburnwhich is out now.

Our debut single Slowburn came out in April 2026, along with a music video directed by Ansel Tan. More is coming.

For press inquiries, booking, or anything else: luxurysportsedanband@gmail.com
